## Full description (max 4000 chars)

> **The day's developer news. Skimmed.**
>
> Skimbyte reads the developer-news firehose and turns it into a once-a-day brief of
> short, readable summaries — finish it with your coffee instead of doomscrolling a
> dozen feeds.
>
> **Pick your topics.** Kotlin, Android, KMP / Compose Multiplatform, iOS & Swift,
> Flutter & Dart, React Native, AI for mobile devs, tooling & products, XR — follow
> what you build, skip what you don't.
>
> **One brief at a time.** Each story is a card: a one-minute summary, the source,
> and the read time. Swipe to the next, or jump to the full article when it's worth
> your attention.
>
> **Bookmark the keepers.** Sign in with Google or GitHub to sync bookmarks, topics,
> and reading stats across your devices — phone, desktop, and web.
>
> **Make it yours.** Matte black or off-white theme and ten sharp accent colors.
>
> **No ads. No tracking.** Skimbyte is free and open source (Apache-2.0). There are
> no ads, no analytics, no engagement tricks — just the news, summarized. The entire
> app is on GitHub, and it even runs in demo mode without an account.
>
> **One codebase, every platform.** Skimbyte is built with Kotlin Multiplatform and
> Compose Multiplatform — the same code ships natively on Android, iOS, macOS,
> Windows, Linux, and the web. Developers: clone it, run it, learn from it.
>
> Website: https://skimbyte.com
> Source: https://github.com/redigitsoft/Skimbyte

## Notes

- Icon: Play requires the full-square 512×512 (`icon-512.png`); Google applies its own mask.
- Feature graphic: 1024×500, shown behind the play button on the listing.
- A bookmarks-screen panel is worth adding later from a signed-in device (demo mode
  gates bookmarks behind sign-in, so it couldn't be captured locally).
- If the app targets tablets, Play also wants 7"/10" tablet screenshots — capture at
  a tablet window size the same way (see marketing/README.md).
