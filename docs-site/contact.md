# Contact

**Point of contact: Akshay — [akshay@redigit.io](mailto:akshay@redigit.io)**

Skimbyte is built and maintained by Redigit. Whatever you need — help with the app, a
question about your account or data, or anything about the project — the routes below
all reach a human.

## Support

Something not working, or a question about using the app?

- **Email:** [akshay@redigit.io](mailto:akshay@redigit.io) — include your platform
  (Android, iOS, desktop, web) and app version (Settings shows it) so we can help faster.
- **Bug reports & feature requests:** open an issue on the public tracker at
  [github.com/RedigitSoft/Skimbyte/issues](https://github.com/RedigitSoft/Skimbyte/issues).

## Privacy and account requests

Account deletion is built into the app (**Account → Delete my data**) and takes effect
immediately — no email needed. For anything else about your data, see the
[privacy policy](privacy.md) or email [akshay@redigit.io](mailto:akshay@redigit.io).

## Security

Found a vulnerability? Please report it **privately** to
[akshay@redigit.io](mailto:akshay@redigit.io) rather than opening a public issue, and
allow a reasonable window for a fix before disclosure.

## Everything else

Press, licensing (the app is [Apache-2.0](https://github.com/RedigitSoft/Skimbyte/blob/main/LICENSE)),
or just want to say hi: [akshay@redigit.io](mailto:akshay@redigit.io).
